Venue ROI Rules

Each venue has its own BS Target and ROI Target by DJ fee tier, day of week, and season. When a DJ's fee falls between two tiers, the dashboard uses the nearest one. If the fee doesn't exactly match a tier's anchor fee, the BS Target stays fixed and the ROI Target is recalculated so the dollar target owed stays the same.
